# Surface Brief: src/App.vue

- **Scope:** Full application (`src/App.vue` and all PT-BR components in `src/components/`)
- **Visitor Mode:** Operate

## Audience & Scene
Players gathered around a physical table (bar, barbecue, court, living room) using a phone flat on the table or held one-handed, or a desktop screen visible from across the room.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A tactile tabletop scorekeeper built of weighted lacquered score plates and stamped match slips on a warm dark club table—refusing the generic dark-slate AI SaaS dashboard of translucent glass cards and tiny pills.
OWN-WORLD: Deep walnut-charcoal table surface (`#14110f` and `#1d1815`) with warm brass/ivory hardware, saturated lacquered team plates (`#c8332b` Carmim, `#1e56a0` Azul-Real, `#16794b` Verde-Pano, `#d97706` Ouro, `#6d28d9` Violeta, `#0e7490` Turquesa), stepped physical control tabs, and perforated match-history slips.
STORY: Players configure rules and teams in seconds, tap giant physical score plates to rack up points with floating point bursts and mechanical plate feedback, hear the victory fanfare (`/victory.mp3`), and rotate waiting teams ("quem ganha fica") with one tap.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top rail with tactile game-table tab switches and audio toggle; directly below, either the full-bleed responsive grid of monumental team score plates (in Play mode) or the physical game-box rule & team tray (in Setup mode) anchored by a full-width arcade start bar.
